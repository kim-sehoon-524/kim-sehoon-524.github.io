Privacy Policy (English)

App Name: Alchemy Defense · Effective Date: November 25, 2026

1. Information Stored by the App

Alchemy Defense requires no account registration and does not collect directly identifying personal information (such as your name, phone number, physical address, contacts, or precise location) on developer servers.

  • Gameplay data: Wave progress, unlocked codex entries, currency, missions, research, audio, and language settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Accounts & social features: The app has no account creation, chat, user-generated content, or user-to-user data sharing.

2. Advertising & App Tracking Transparency (Google Mobile Ads)

Alchemy Defense uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to serve optional rewarded and interstitial ads. On iOS, explicit permission is requested via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt before accessing any tracking identifier; declining tracking does not restrict gameplay. Google and its advertising partners may automatically process approximate location (from IP), device/advertising identifiers, app/ad interactions, and crash/diagnostics data for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and compliance. See the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Retention, Deletion & Children's Privacy

You can permanently delete all local gameplay data anytime via the Reset (🔄) button in the app or by uninstalling the app. The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

4. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries, contact k10000co@gmail.com.
