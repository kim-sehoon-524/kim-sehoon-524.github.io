Privacy Policy (English)

App Name: Alchemy Defense · Effective Date: November 23, 2026

1. Information Stored by the App

Alchemy Defense does not require an account and does not send directly identifying information such as your name, phone number, address, contacts, or precise location to the developer’s servers.

  • Gameplay data: Wave progress, unlocked codex entries, currency, missions, research, and audio settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Accounts and social features: The app has no account creation, chat, user-generated content, or user-to-user data sharing.

2. Google Mobile Ads

The Android app and other versions in which advertising is enabled use the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to provide rewarded and interstitial ads. Google and its advertising partners may automatically process the following information for advertising, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance:

  • Approximate location inferred from an IP address
  • Device or other identifiers, including advertising ID and app set ID
  • App interactions and advertising interactions
  • Crash logs, diagnostics, and performance information

For details, see the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

3. Retention and Deletion

You can delete locally stored gameplay data through the Reset (🔄) or Terms & Info → Delete Saved Data option. Uninstalling the app also removes local app data. Information processed by advertising services is governed by Google’s retention and deletion policies.

4. Children’s Privacy

The app is not specifically designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.
